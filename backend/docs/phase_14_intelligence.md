# Phase 14: Advanced Intelligence, Optimization & Autonomous Canteen Operations

## Overview
Phase 14 transforms the canteen ecosystem from a system of record into an intelligent decision-support platform. It introduces a central Intelligence Engine that consumes real-time operational data, predicts anomalies, and generates prescriptive recommendations for human approval.

## Milestones Achieved

### 1. Operational Intelligence Data Layer
- **Models Added:**
  - `IntelligenceSignal`: Captures anomalies and alerts (e.g., `DEMAND_SURGE`, `INVENTORY_ANOMALY`).
  - `Recommendation`: Actionable suggestions generated by the engine (e.g., `REORDER`, `ADJUST_STAFF`).
  - `RecommendationAudit`: Tracks human decisions (Approve, Reject, Modify) and execution outcomes.

### 2. Real-Time Operational Snapshot
- **Implementation:** `IntelligenceService.get_real_time_snapshot()`
- **Endpoint:** `GET /api/v1/intelligence/snapshot`
- **Details:** Aggregates active orders, kitchen utilization percent, orders per minute, and critical stock items to compute a live `operational_health_score`.

### 3. & 4. Stockout Risk Engine
- **Implementation:** `IntelligenceService.generate_stockout_risk_signal()`
- **Details:** Evaluates real-time inventory against current consumption velocity to predict impending stockouts. Generates `REORDER` recommendations.

### 5. Demand Intelligence
- **Implementation:** `IntelligenceService.generate_demand_surge_signal()`
- **Details:** Detects anomalous spikes in product demand (e.g., 300% surge over baseline) and recommends increasing preparation volumes dynamically.

### 6. Kitchen Capacity & Bottleneck Intelligence
- **Implementation:** `IntelligenceService.generate_kitchen_bottleneck_signal()`
- **Details:** Monitors active fulfillments per stall. If the load exceeds comfortable limits, it flags a bottleneck and recommends staff re-allocation (`ADJUST_STAFF`).

### 7. Supplier Risk Intelligence
- **Implementation:** `IntelligenceService.generate_supplier_risk_signal()`
- **Details:** Scans the active supply chain for critical products. If all suppliers exhibit low reliability or long lead times, it flags a `SUPPLIER_ANOMALY` to preemptively secure alternative sources.

### 8. & 9. Recommendation Engine & Human Approval Workflow
- **Implementation:** `POST /api/v1/intelligence/recommendations/<rec_id>/decide`
- **Details:** The system does not act autonomously by default. It generates a queue of recommendations. A Canteen Manager reviews and acts upon them (Approve, Reject, Modify). The system logs this to the `RecommendationAudit` table to train future ML loops.

### 10. What-If Simulation
- **Implementation:** `IntelligenceService.simulate_scenario()`, `POST /api/v1/intelligence/simulate`
- **Details:** Allows managers to inject hypothetical parameters (e.g., "What if demand triples?") and projects the immediate impact on inventory depletion and kitchen capacity.

### 11. & 12. Command Center & Benchmarking
- **Implementation:** `GET /api/v1/intelligence/benchmark`
- **Details:** Provides a cross-canteen view of stall performance, evaluating active load vs total orders to determine real-time operational health (Healthy, Warning, Critical) across the campus.

## Evaluation Loop
- **Implementation:** `POST /api/v1/intelligence/evaluate`
- **Details:** Serves as the trigger mechanism (typically called by a CRON job or outbox event processor) to scan the entire ecosystem and generate new signals and recommendations.
